# 📖 RealtyAI — User Guide

A practical guide to getting the most out of all four RealtyAI tools.

---

## Getting Started

1. Open **RealEstateAI.html** in your browser
2. Paste your Anthropic API key into the **API Key** field at the top and click **Save Key**
3. Pick a tool from the left sidebar
4. Fill in the input box and click **Generate**

Your result appears in the output box — edit it freely, then copy it wherever you need it.

---

## The Four Tools

---

### 🏡 Tool 1 — Listing Description

**What it does:** Turns your raw property notes into polished, MLS-ready copy in under 10 seconds.

**How to use it:**
1. Click **Listing Description** in the left sidebar
2. Type or paste your property details into the **Property Details** box
3. Click **✦ Generate Listing Description**
4. Your description appears in the output box — edit it, then copy it to your MLS

**What to include for best results:**
- Bed and bath count, square footage
- Standout features (views, renovations, unique architecture)
- Neighborhood context (walkability, schools, nearby amenities)
- Recent upgrades (roof, HVAC, kitchen)
- Year built

**Example input:**
```
3BR/2BA craftsman bungalow, 1,450 sqft. Updated kitchen with quartz counters,
hardwood floors throughout, large backyard with mature oak tree. Walk to downtown
shops. Built 1928, new roof 2021.
```

---

### 💬 Tool 2 — Lead Reply

**What it does:** Drafts a warm, professional email reply to a buyer or seller inquiry in seconds.

**How to use it:**
1. Click **Lead Reply** in the left sidebar
2. Paste the buyer or seller's message into the input box
3. Click **Generate Lead Reply**
4. Edit the draft as needed and send

**What to include for best results:**
- The full inquiry text
- Their name if they signed it
- Any context you want reflected (e.g. whether showings are available, if price is flexible)

**Example input:**
```
Hi, I saw the listing at 412 Maple St. Is the price negotiable?
We have 3 kids so school district is really important to us.
Can we schedule a weekend showing? — The Johnsons
```

---

### 📋 Tool 3 — Client Summary

**What it does:** Converts messy call notes into a clean, structured CRM brief with sections for budget, timeline, target areas, must-haves, deal-breakers, and more.

**How to use it:**
1. Click **Client Summary** in the left sidebar
2. Paste your raw notes — they can be as messy as you like
3. Click **Generate Client Summary**
4. Copy the structured brief into your CRM

**What to include for best results:**
- Budget and pre-approval amount
- Location preferences
- Timeline and current situation (renting, selling first, etc.)
- Must-haves and deal-breakers
- Anything notable from the conversation

**Example input:**
```
Call with Karen M. Looking for 3-4 bed in Pasadena or Arcadia, max $950k.
Must have good school district, twins starting K next year. Wants move-in ready.
Currently renting, lease ends Aug 31st. Pre-approved $900k. Loves natural light.
Hates busy streets and small kitchens.
```

---

### ✉️ Tool 4 — Marketing Email

**What it does:** Writes a campaign-ready listing email with a subject line, property highlights, and a call to action.

**How to use it:**
1. Click **Marketing Email** in the left sidebar
2. Paste your listing details into the input box
3. Click **Generate Marketing Email**
4. Copy the subject line and body into your email platform

**What to include for best results:**
- Property address
- Price
- Top 2–3 standout features
- Open house date and time (if applicable)

**Example input:**
```
New listing at 88 Sycamore Dr, San Marino. 4BR/3BA, 2,800 sqft.
Completely renovated, chef's kitchen, resort-style pool.
Listed at $1.85M. Open house this Sunday 1–4pm.
```

---

## Tips for Better Results

- **Be specific** — the more detail you give, the better the output
- **Edit freely** — every result is a starting point, not a final draft. Add your voice before publishing
- **Re-generate if needed** — not happy with the first result? Click Generate again for a fresh version
- **Your key is safe** — your API key and all your input data stay in your browser. Nothing is stored on any server
